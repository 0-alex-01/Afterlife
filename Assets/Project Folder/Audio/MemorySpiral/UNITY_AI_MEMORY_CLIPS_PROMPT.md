# Unity AI prompt — Tree memory clips

```text
Create one cohesive set of five original, non-musical cinematic memory sound clips for a contemplative VR afterlife scene. The player climbs an ancient tree; at four platforms, physical fragments assemble into brief symbolic images, then all memories merge into a luminous crown and rise toward a portal. The sound should feel intimate, humane, melancholic, and increasingly peaceful — never horror, fantasy-combat, sentimental trailer music, or a jump scare.

DELIVERY
- Export five separate one-shot WAV files with the exact filenames below.
- Clips 01–04: exactly 10.0 seconds each. Clip 05: exactly 8.0 seconds.
- 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono for Unity 3D spatialization. If mono is unavailable, use identical dual-mono with no stereo movement.
- Match all files to approximately -22 LUFS integrated, maximum -3 dBTP, with comparable perceived loudness.
- Dry and centered, only a very short natural tail; no baked cavern reverb. Leave the spatial reverb to Unity.
- Clean 120–250 ms fade-in and 350–700 ms fade-out; no clicks and no tail beyond the file.
- Use a clear low/mid/high-frequency gesture in every clip so real-time spectrum analysis can drive fragment pulse, drift, and sparks.
- No intelligible words, no recognizable melody, no copyrighted motif, no child crying, no uncanny reversed speech, no sudden transient louder than the rest.

01 — Memory_01_Birth_Childhood.wav
An intimate first-memory texture: a soft heartbeat-like wooden pulse, warm cloth movement, quiet breath, a tiny seed-like shimmer, and one delicate bell glint near the middle. Start fragile and close, briefly open into safety and wonder, then settle. Strong but gentle low-frequency pulse, warm low mids, sparse high glints. The image it drives is a cradle holding a luminous life seed.

02 — Memory_02_Voices_Laughter.wav
Three or four distant human presences remembered through warmth rather than language: soft nonverbal vowels, breath, restrained natural laughter, and hand-like rhythmic taps. Friendly and imperfect, never ghostly or uncanny. Create conversational mid-frequency pulses that seem to answer one another, with a few light high-frequency laughter sparks. The image it drives is a circle of connected people.

03 — Memory_03_Nature_Breath.wav
A remembered forest inhaling: close leaves, soft wind through bark, one subtle wing movement, a distant non-specific bird tone, and a low wooden resonance. Organic, calm, and tactile, with controlled high-frequency leaf detail and a slow breathing envelope. No generic jungle ambience and no prominent bird call. The image it drives is a bird emerging from leaves.

04 — Memory_04_Harmony.wav
The first three sound worlds gradually agree: wooden heartbeat, human breath, leaf texture, soft glass resonance, and a very restrained wordless choir color converge into one balanced harmonic bloom. Begin sparse, grow across the full spectrum, reach a warm luminous crest around 7.5 seconds, then leave a clear breath of silence. No epic trailer swell. The image it drives is a branching tree constellation.

05 — Memory_05_Ascension_Synthesis.wav
A final upward synthesis that recalls tiny details from all four previous clips without becoming a song: one deep wooden pulse, a trace of shared breath, leaf lift, glass shimmer, then a rising airy spiral. Build toward a soft golden portal chime around 6.5 seconds and resolve into peace, not triumph. Give the ascent readable mid/high motion for sparks while preserving a calm low foundation. The image it drives is a luminous fragment crown rising into the portal.

Make the five clips feel recorded and mastered in the same acoustic world, with a clear emotional progression: fragile life -> human connection -> nature -> inner harmony -> release.
```
